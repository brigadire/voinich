Evidence / Historical dictionaries

Matched brute-force comparison · f68r2

A negative dictionary result, with its controls exposed.

Historical astronomical and botanical dictionaries were tested against the same 27 frozen LABEL occurrences. The search did not identify a domain-specific signal within the tested global-form model class.

02 · SEPARATE POSITIVE RESULT

Hapax enrichment is not a dictionary match.

A separate frozen analysis found 80 section-local hapaxes among 112 independently matched astronomical LABEL occurrences: 0.714285714 versus a null mean of 0.611207143, enrichment ratio 1.168647524, one-sided permutation p=0.008799120. All 8/8 leave-one-panel-out exclusions retained positive direction and p<0.05.

This is a structural token-frequency result. It does not say that every hapax is a label, does not identify meanings and does not constitute a decipherment.

The 112-occurrence analysis covers eight named panels and all astronomical label families. It differs from the earlier restricted pilot (57 STAR LABEL occurrences on f68r1/f68r2) and from this search’s 27 frozen f68r2 labels. See the scope table.

03 · CONTROLS

The null distribution is visible.

Among 99 pseudo-controls, 72 reached maximum 0, 11 reached 2 and 16 reached 3. The historical control reached 3/27 with empirical p=0.17; it is not a domain-specific signal. This is why the botanical and astronomical zero maxima do not support a special-domain interpretation.

04 · BOUNDARY

What this does not show

The negative result applies only to the tested corrected-global form-matching model, panels, profiles and controls. It does not disprove botanical, astronomical or any other page theme. No plaintext, word identification or thematic interpretation follows.

The current global-substitution branch is closed. Any future work requires an independently justified abbreviation, mnemonic/notational or morpheme-compositional model, frozen before results are viewed.
