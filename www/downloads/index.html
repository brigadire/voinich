AVAILABILITY

What is—and is not—distributed

Public now

Thirty reviewed artifacts, including the complete structural catalog bundle, plus site pages, checksums, release manifest and changelog.

Third-party authority

ZL3b/IT2a transcription data, IVTT, the Burchards Dekret Digital source and some controls are obtained from their named authorities under their terms.

Restricted pending review

Other unreviewed outputs, explorations, operational details, prompts and internal research material.

Planned repository release

Source code and additional frozen evidence after review. No date is asserted by this package.

Not distributable here

Third-party corpus bytes where redistribution rights are not established. Their identities and checksums remain documented.

Not available as PDF

No frozen PDF artifact exists in this release. Original Markdown and machine-readable tables are provided; browser print remains editorial output.

Links inside copied original Markdown files remain repository-relative provenance references. If a referenced low-level artifact is not in this curated package, the authoritative artifact index records its role for the planned repository release; its absence here is not evidence suppression.
