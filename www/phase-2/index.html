Research / Phase II

External memory and identifiability

Mechanism classes were tested. None was identified.

Phase II transformed historical and conceptual analogies into frozen mechanism classes, then tested whether their outputs cover the manuscript’s expanded structural fingerprint.

DEFINITION

External memory, operationalized

A structured record that works together with conventions, context, path, index or knowledge retained outside the record. Phase I had shown that surface organization does not determine recoverability, leaving this context-dependent class open. The experiments measured external state, knowledge dependence, recovery level, ambiguity, collisions and information behavior—not metaphorical resemblance.

Why Fontana. Giovanni Fontana’s ca. 1430 Secretum describes concrete cue, convention, ordered-path, index and association systems from approximately the Voynich Manuscript’s historical period. It therefore supplied historically bounded mechanism families that could be formalized without inventing an arbitrary modern mnemonic model. Fontana is a source of experimental mechanisms, not evidence of authorship, contact, influence or common origin.

The comparison concerns model outputs: it asks whether Fontana-derived mechanisms reproduce selected fingerprint properties—not whether the Voynich Manuscript “looks like” the Secretum.

  1. Natural text COMPATIBLE · PARTIAL

    Descriptively closest in the available direct space, but it failed the frozen multi-family gate. Visible-token linguistic interpretation, semantics and formal-language models remain untested.

  2. External memory COMPATIBLE · LEVEL_1

    Fontana-derived models, used as one operationalized family of external-memory mechanisms, retained limited endpoint resemblance within the edit-metric family. There was no valid frozen before/after trajectory and no null-separated, multi-family advantage.

    This result does not generalize to all possible external-memory systems.

  3. BDD historical abbreviation DISFAVORED · S0

    Real trajectory cosine was 0.279072 / 0.262481 versus matched deletions around 0.99; minimum shorthand-null p-value was 0.25. This applies to one tested BDD tradition, not all shorthand.

  4. Selective extraction COMPATIBLE · A1

    Some operators reached directional cosine 0.803801, but none separated from matched nulls (minimum p=0.142857). FIRST/LAST collapse line structure mechanically; AX was not validated. No hidden channel was detected.

INTEGRITY

A failed run was preserved as a negative result

The initial confirmatory comparison was invalidated after an IT2a checksum inconsistency and incomplete transitive audit exposed historical nondeterminism. It was quarantined and never used as final comparison evidence. A deterministic reconstruction then refroze the fingerprint: RUN_A/B/C produced 75 byte-identical normative files; 10 of 66 metric rows changed, while all 13 CORE statuses remained unchanged. The corrected confirmatory comparison ran from that freeze.
