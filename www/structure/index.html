Research / Structural property catalog

Descriptive · empirical · corpus-bounded

A grammar of observations—and absences.

This catalog records concrete structural relations in the canonical ZL3b transcription: which symbol combinations occur, which never occur, how tokens follow and share lines, and where forms concentrate across the document hierarchy.

01 · CORPUS

The measured surface

Literal transcription symbols are analyzed without redefining them as physical atomic glyphs, letters or linguistic units. Physical lines remain hard boundaries. ZL3b supplies the primary catalog; IT2a supplies an independent stability check.

39,380token occurrences
8,244unique token types
5,385physical lines

Scope boundary. These are facts about the ZL3b canonical corpus. “Never observed” is not rewritten as “insufficient evidence,” but it is also not promoted to “impossible in Voynichese” or to a claim about the generating mechanism.

Concrete positional facts

0, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8, 9 and b are not observed token-initially. Every literal symbol is observed token-finally at least once. The familiar q is overwhelmingly initial—5,401 of 5,436 occurrences—but it has 13 token-final occurrences, all accounted for by single-symbol tokens. Within-token q→o occurs 5,301 times; many alternatives after q remain explicit observed zeros.

03 · TOKENS

Formation and sequencing remain distinct.

Prefix and suffix patterns are descriptive string patterns, not morphemes. Edit-distance-one families are graph components, not claims of morphological derivation.

Dominant edge patterns

Highest-frequency initial patterns include o (8,939), c (7,063), ch (6,141), q (5,401) and qo (5,269). Frequent terminal patterns include y (15,732), dy (6,821), n (6,093), l (6,000) and in (5,958).

Edit geometry

The literal Levenshtein-distance-one graph contains 29,923 edges and 1,183 connected components across all token types. Insertions, deletions and substitutions are cataloged with positions, support and examples.

05 · LINES

Adjacency is not co-occurrence.

The line catalog separately records first and last tokens, normalized position, token and glyph counts, repetition, token-length progression and edit-family progression. The co-occurrence null retains token frequencies and every physical-line slot length. Seventeen same-line pairs are statistically avoided or depleted after BH-FDR; every other zero remains a corpus rule even when sampling explains it.

06 · DOCUMENT

Structure continues above the line.

Tokens and edit families are crossed with four locus types, 227 folios and eight sections. Every category absence remains visible; enrichment and depletion are assessed separately.

A concrete section profile

daiin occurs 851 times. It is strongly preferred in sections H (460 observed; 253.36 expected) and P (98 observed; 56.79 expected), and depleted in B, C, S, T and Z under the frozen document-category null.

Independent transcription

Key symbol-position, bigram, token-transition, line-position and same-line relations are compared with IT2a as SAME, DIFFERENT or NOT_COMPARABLE. Literal identity is not invented where transcription systems diverge.
