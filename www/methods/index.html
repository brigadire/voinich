Evidence / Methods

Bounded reproducibility

Verify the chain, not a reconstructed manuscript.

The public package lets readers audit report-to-table consistency, corpus identities, freezes, checksums and deterministic reruns. It does not claim that token-stream models reproduce the manuscript as a material and spatial object.

NATURAL-LANGUAGE CONTROL

Why Doyle—and exactly which Doyle

WORKFLOW

Frozen evidence discipline

01 · DEFINE

Questions and measurement contracts

Analytical units, metric families, thresholds, missingness and support gates were declared for each experiment.

02 · FREEZE

Target-blind mechanisms and controls

Mechanism portfolios were frozen before Voynich target access; seeds were independently derived.

03 · VALIDATE

Synthetic gates and held-out controls

Methods that failed their gate were not promoted to Voynich inference. Null, blocked and invalid results remain visible.

04 · COMPARE

Family-balanced direct space

The corrected confirmatory comparison used common direct metrics, pre-target natural-control normalization, family aggregation, explicit missingness and both VM transcriptions.

05 · AUDIT

Checksum provenance and deterministic refreeze

The initial comparison was quarantined after failing its provenance audit. The corrected deterministic reconstruction produced byte-identical A/B/C outputs before the confirmatory comparison opened the target.

STRUCTURAL CATALOG

Corpus facts and inference are separate fields.

Observed layer

Every enumerated relation stores its count, opportunity count, probability and an independent OBSERVED/UNOBSERVED status. A zero is never filtered out because it is statistically unsurprising.

Inferred layer

Directional analytic nulls retain the relevant lower-level marginals. BH-FDR changes only the inferred classification—expected, preferred, depleted or avoided—not the corpus fact.

Matched structure

Glyph adjacency uses within-token slots; transitions retain hard line boundaries and predecessor/successor marginals; co-occurrence retains token frequencies and every physical-line length; document tests retain category sizes.

Stability and reproducibility

Key rules are checked independently against IT2a. Two complete ZL3b generations produced byte-identical manifests. The public deterministic bundle includes all tables and their internal checksums.

VERIFY

Public package checks

  1. Verify file integrity

    Download the bundle contents and compare SHA-256 values with RELEASE_MANIFEST.json or SHA256SUMS.

  2. Check human/machine parity

    Terminal statuses and numerical claims on this site can be compared with the original Phase II TSV registry and JSON results manifest.

  3. Reconstruct external inputs if needed

    The data guide records authoritative sources, hashes and preparation commands. Corpus bytes with unclear redistribution rights are deliberately absent.

  4. Distinguish audit from full rerun

    The package supports provenance and internal-consistency verification. Full experiment reruns require the future repository release, exact third-party inputs and documented software.
