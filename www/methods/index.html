Evidence / Methods

Bounded reproducibility

Verify the chain, not a reconstructed manuscript.

The public package lets readers audit report-to-table consistency, corpus identities, freezes, checksums and deterministic reruns. It does not claim that token-stream models reproduce the manuscript as a material and spatial object.

Authority and transcription handling

Zandbergen–Landini ZL3b IVTFF, converted with historical IVTT -x7. Phase I baseline derivative SHA-256: 360d99583145…87c2. Phase II binds exact inputs in the deterministic V2.1 manifest.

THIRD-PARTY · NOT REDISTRIBUTED

IT2a · independent check

Takahashi IT2a transliteration via Jorge Stolfi’s 1999 interlinear file, IVTFF 2.0. Used for cross-transcription status/direction stability in Fingerprint V2.1.

INDEPENDENT TRANSCRIPTION CHECK

Natural controls include Doyle and Longfellow; historical tasks also used Astafiev. Experiment-specific controls include invariant-preserving shuffles, known transformations, structured positive controls, copy/mutate generators, stationary/drift/state simulations, C-GRAMMAR, matched deletions and matched subsequences. Each null answers only the question defined by what it preserves and destroys.

WORKFLOW

Frozen evidence discipline

01 · DEFINE

Questions and measurement contracts

Analytical units, metric families, thresholds, missingness and support gates were declared at task level.

02 · FREEZE

Target-blind mechanisms and controls

Mechanism portfolios were frozen before Voynich target access; seeds were independently derived.

03 · VALIDATE

Synthetic gates and held-out controls

Methods that failed their gate were not promoted to Voynich inference. Null, blocked and invalid results remain visible.

04 · COMPARE

Family-balanced direct space

Task83r used common direct metrics, pre-target natural-control normalization, family aggregation, explicit missingness and both VM transcriptions.

05 · AUDIT

Checksum provenance and deterministic refreeze

The invalid Task83 comparison was quarantined. The corrected Task83b reconstruction produced byte-identical A/B/C outputs before Task83r opened the target.

EVIDENCE TYPES

Five layers kept separate

1

Observation

A stored measurement under a declared corpus and estimator.

2

Statistical inference

A comparison against the specific frozen control or gate.

3

Mechanistic interpretation

Compatibility with a model class, without identification.

4

Historical interpretation

Requires independent source and provenance evidence.

5

Future question

Transferred to Phase III; not a present result.

VERIFY

Public package checks

  1. Verify file integrity

    Download the bundle contents and compare SHA-256 values with RELEASE_MANIFEST.json or SHA256SUMS.

  2. Check human/machine parity

    Terminal statuses and numerical claims on this site can be compared with the original Phase II TSV registry and Task84 JSON manifest.

  3. Reconstruct external inputs if needed

    The data guide records authoritative sources, hashes and preparation commands. Corpus bytes with unclear redistribution rights are deliberately absent.

  4. Distinguish audit from full rerun

    The package supports provenance and internal-consistency verification. Full experiment reruns require the future repository release, exact third-party inputs and documented software.
