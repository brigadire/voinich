Research / Phase I

Measurement and constraint

What structure is actually there?

Phase I established a multiscale structural baseline, tested narrow mechanisms, and kept recoverability separate from surface resemblance.

QUESTION

Scope and corpus

The baseline used the Zandbergen–Landini ZL3b-x7 transcription projection (SHA-256 360d9958…87c2; 39,026 tokens in later baseline analyses). The positional, repetition, entropy, formation, transition, local-regime, mechanism-space and recovery studies used a separately parsed 39,380-token corpus (SHA-256 f46f4190…692). The two corpora are not silently pooled.

Unit discipline. “Glyph”, “token”, “line”, “page” and “regime” name transcription or analytical units. They do not assert letters, words, sentences, topics, plaintext blocks or keys.

Why Doyle appears here. Arthur Conan Doyle’s The Sign of Four was used only as a known natural-language control corpus. It provides a reproducible English text against which the behaviour of the same estimators can be checked. Doyle is not proposed as a historical source, linguistic analogue, plaintext candidate, or model of the Voynich Manuscript. The comparison shows how a metric behaves on one ordinary natural-language corpus; it does not establish a universal natural-language baseline.

PRINCIPAL RESULTS

Measured observations and bounded inferences

  1. Token formation is strongly constrained SUPPORTED

    Six frequent transcription glyphs were near-strict initial, medial or final specialists at frozen thresholds. Simple position-independent homophony controls produced zero such specialists.

    Scope: canonical 39,380-token parse; within-token positions; corrected position-independent homophony controls.

  2. Visible token order is weak; edges remain coupled OBSERVED

    Corrected adjacent-token mutual information was 0.090736 bits (1.099% of capped token entropy), while corrected edge-glyph MI was 0.216302 bits (6.271%).

    Interpretation: ordinary-word treatment of visible tokens is strained; this is not evidence of no message.

  3. Local form neighborhoods survive controls SUPPORTED

    Exact adjacent repetition was explained by the line-preserving null, but near-edit repetition remained enriched after line, length and frequency matching. Edit distance is formal similarity, not demonstrated morphology.

  4. Predictability depends on representation PARTIALLY_SUPPORTED

    Under the declared continuous-stream estimator, the Voynich transcription yielded h2 = 2.7726 bits, compared with 3.5978 bits for the selected Doyle control. Boundary-symbol and within-token estimates changed materially.

    The difference is descriptive and estimator-specific: it neither identifies the Voynich system nor demonstrates that it is non-linguistic.

  5. Organization extends beyond a line SUPPORTED

    Local effects vary heterogeneously across manuscript regions, combining drift-like and state-like structure. No topic, key schedule or production episode was identified.

  6. Surface fit does not determine recovery SUPPORTED

    Known-plaintext synthetic tests separated reversibility, exact recovery, retained information, ambiguity and robustness. No result establishes that the Voynich Manuscript itself is intrinsically lossy.

NEGATIVE RESULTS

What weakened—and what did not

Inverse transposition

The selected width-2 natural inverse worsened all four holdout structural metrics.

NARROW FAMILY DISFAVORED

Other layouts, widths, compound systems and plaintext recovery were not excluded.

Inverse homophony

The synthetic structural-recovery gate passed only 3/9 cases. Voynich analysis was NOT_RUN_BY_DESIGN.

METHOD GATE FAILED

This says nothing for or against Voynich homophony.

Mechanism grid

Constrained formation was required; homophony and stochastic output were disfavored as main explanations in the finite grid.

SCOPED RESULT

The grid does not exhaust complex transformations or generators.

CONCLUSION

Three broad classes remained open

H_L

Natural or formal symbolic language

Viable, not selected.

H_C

Complex meaningful transformation

Viable; simple variants disfavored; not selected.

H_G

Structured message-free generation

Viable, not selected.
