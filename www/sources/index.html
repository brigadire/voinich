Primary historical source

Giovanni Fontana, Secretum

Secretum de thesauro experimentorum ymaginationis hominum, ca. 1430. Paris, BnF, NAL 635. Source authority for images, layout and manuscript readings; the surviving copy is not an autograph.

Used to motivate bounded mechanism families—not Voynich authorship.

Paired historical data

Köln, Cod. 119, Burchards Dekret Digital TEI edition, commit 29f9cb1…, CC BY 4.0. Paired <abbr>/<expan> source for the historical-abbreviation analysis.

One manuscript, scribe and abbreviation tradition.
