Phase I · surface structure

A constrained symbolic system

Glyph placement, token formation, edit geometry, repetition and local organization impose joint constraints. Several simple mechanisms were inadequate, but no broad generating class was selected.

Phase II · mechanism classes

An exact evidential bottleneck

Phase I showed that surface structure alone could not determine how information was stored or recovered. Phase II therefore tested generating and recovery classes, including systems in which a visible record depends on external convention, context or remembered knowledge. Historically grounded examples of such mechanisms were derived from Giovanni Fontana’s Secretum.

None passed its frozen support gate, and the available metric intersection was insufficient to identify a mechanism.

03 · RESULTS

The result is a boundary, not a reveal

Natural text was descriptively closest in the available direct space. It still failed the frozen multi-family support gate. External memory retained partial, one-family compatibility. Neither proximity identifies a mechanism.
