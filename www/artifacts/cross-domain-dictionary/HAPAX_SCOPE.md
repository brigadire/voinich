# Scope of the two label analyses

The 80/112 hapax result and the 26/57 restricted pilot are different analyses, not contradictory denominators.

| analysis | pages/panels | unit | N | purpose |
|---|---|---|---:|---|
| Hapax enrichment | f67r1, f67r2, f67v1, f68r1, f68r2, f68r3, f68v2, f68v1 | independently confirmed astronomical `LABEL` occurrence | 112 | primary structural enrichment test |
| Dictionary search | f68r2 | frozen `LABEL` occurrence | 27 | matched historical-dictionary form search |
| Restricted pilot | f68r1 and f68r2 | `STAR LABEL` occurrence | 57 | earlier restricted robustness/pilot analysis |

The primary 112-occurrence inventory includes astronomical label families in the eight named panels, not only STAR labels. The restricted 57-occurrence pilot is STAR-only on f68r1/f68r2. The f68r2 dictionary search uses the 27 frozen labels for that panel and is not a resampling of the 112-occurrence hapax test.

`Section-local hapax` means a token whose frequency is exactly one among the 901 frozen occurrences in metadata section `A` (the Astronomical section). Introductory prose and circular annotations are not silently added to that sampling frame; the separate restricted pilot reports those controls explicitly. Confirmed labels are deduplicated by absolute token position, and no unmatched-record compensation or post-hoc inventory completion is used.
