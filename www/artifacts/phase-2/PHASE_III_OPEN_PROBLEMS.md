# Phase III open scientific problems

This document states problems implied by Phase II. It is not a Task85+ roadmap and specifies no implementation sequence.

1. **Formal grammar.** Can a formal grammar of Voynichese be inferred under held-out evaluation, rather than inferred from marginal token similarity?
2. **Minimality.** What is the smallest grammar that jointly reproduces constrained token formation, edit/lexical structure, and transitions?
3. **Information residual.** After conditioning on that grammar, what reproducible residual dependence remains across tokens, lines, pages, folios, and sections?
4. **Meaningful versus message-free formal systems.** Can two systems matched for grammar and surface statistics be distinguished by information-bearing or predictive tests without assuming a plaintext?
5. **Synthetic manuscript.** Can a model generate a manuscript object rather than only a token stream, with physical line, page, folio, locus, and boundary provenance?
6. **Hierarchy and geometry.** Can it reproduce page/folio hierarchy, recto/verso coherence, local regimes, positional structure, and 2D/manuscript organization without target leakage?
7. **Coverage expansion.** Would such a model increase the direct intersection from 3/13 edit-family CORE metrics to genuine multi-family coverage?
8. **Identification.** With expanded direct coverage, do frozen support and pairwise gates identify a generating class, or does non-identifiability persist?

These questions do not presuppose natural language, cipher, external memory, shorthand, or meaningless generation. Each remains a model class to be operationalized and tested at matching structural scale.
