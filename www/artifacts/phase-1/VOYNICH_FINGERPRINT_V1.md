## Joint reading

The fingerprint is the conjunction, not any single row: strong token-internal constraints coexist with weak mean token-identity order, stronger edge coupling, enriched local edit geometry, representation-sensitive low glyph entropy, modest sequence residuals, and heterogeneous local regimes. A candidate mechanism matching only Zipf-like marginals, entropy, or repetition is not thereby compatible with v1.

No row is a semantic measurement. No row makes recoverability identifiable from the transcription alone.
