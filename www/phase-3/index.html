Research / Phase III boundary

Open questions · not mechanism results

The next tests begin where coverage ends.

This page states the frozen open mechanism problems implied by Phase II. The separately published structural property catalog is a descriptive corpus resource, not a Phase III mechanism-identification experiment and not a solution to the formal-grammar problem.

NO PHASE III MECHANISM RESULT

  1. Formal grammar

    Can a Voynichese grammar be inferred under held-out evaluation rather than from marginal token similarity?

  2. Minimality

    What is the smallest grammar that jointly reproduces constrained formation, edit/lexical structure and transitions?

  3. Information residual

    After conditioning on grammar, what reproducible dependence remains across tokens, lines, pages, folios and sections?

  4. Meaningful versus message-free systems

    Can grammar-matched systems be distinguished by information-bearing or predictive tests without assuming plaintext?

  5. Synthetic manuscript

    Can a model generate a manuscript object—with physical-line, page, folio, locus and boundary provenance—rather than only a token stream?

  6. Hierarchy and geometry

    Can it reproduce page/folio hierarchy, recto/verso coherence, local regimes, positional structure and 2D organization without target leakage?

  7. Coverage expansion

    Can direct coverage grow from 3/13 edit-family CORE metrics to genuine multi-family coverage?

  8. Identification

    With expanded coverage, do frozen support and pairwise gates identify a class—or does non-identifiability persist?

These questions presuppose none of natural language, cipher, external memory, shorthand or meaningless generation. Each remains a model class to be operationalized and tested at matching structural scale.
