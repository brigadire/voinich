    Scientific experiment is fixed, but execution identity is
    genuinely ambiguous.

    Action:
        STOP before production freeze.
        Document exact ambiguity.
        Do not choose arbitrarily.


WORKFLOW_BOUNDARY_DEFECT

    Contract is unambiguous, but the task ordering requires an
    artifact before its legitimate prerequisites exist.

    Action:
        correct workflow locally;
        do not revise scientific contract.


IMPLEMENTATION_DEFECT

    Code disagrees with the unambiguous frozen contract.

    Action:
        fix code;
        rerun validation;
        no contract revision.
