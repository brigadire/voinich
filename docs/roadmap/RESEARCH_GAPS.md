# Research gaps

The literature and evidence matrix support **PARTIALLY_DISTINGUISHABLE**: several results reject narrow models (especially ordinary visible word-token language and simple position-independent homophony), but no result cleanly separates H_L, H_C, and H_G as broad classes.

| Priority | Minimal high-value experiment | Why it discriminates |
|---|---|---|
| HIGH | Pre-register a complete multiscale metric suite, freeze it, then compare matched natural-language writing, specified cipher/encoding systems, and message-free generators on held-out metrics. | Existing forward models reproduce selected properties, leaving selection/tuning ambiguity. |
| HIGH | Run blinded, held-out text–illustration/label association tests while conditioning on Currier class, hand, section, page, and local adjacency; include conditioned non-message generators. | This tests external-content organization rather than language-like statistics. |
| HIGH | For every proposed encoding model, use known plaintext to measure invertibility, preimage multiplicity, key/table dependence, and error/segmentation sensitivity; require a held-out Voynich prediction before applying it to the manuscript. | Separates statistical similarity from retained message information. |
| MEDIUM | Repeat core entropy, MI, morphology, and positional results across EVA, Currier, Zandbergen–Landini, and explicit ambiguous-glyph/space perturbations. | Determines whether signals are artifacts of transcription/segmentation. |
| MEDIUM | Fit only models selected without Voynich target tuning; test their full out-of-sample fingerprint and transfer across languages/genres. | Distinguishes general mechanisms from target-fitted simulations. |
| MEDIUM | Release a hand/Currier/section/page aligned corpus with uncertainty annotations and analyse residual structure under blocked resampling. | Resolves metadata and local-regime confounding. |
| LOW | Digitize and publish primary Currier/Friedman materials with stable citations. | Improves historical traceability but is less immediately discriminating. |
