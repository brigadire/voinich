# Phase II executive summary

Phase II began because Phase I established a strongly structured Voynich Manuscript (VM) transcription but could not distinguish complex meaningful transformation, natural/artificial formal language, and structured message-free generation. Synthetic work also showed that statistical similarity, plaintext dependence, reversibility, information retention, and autonomous recovery are different. This motivated a specific question: could the VM be an **external-memory system**, where a structured record works with knowledge or context retained outside the record?

Phase II expanded the target into Fingerprint V2, studied Giovanni Fontana’s *Secretum* as a historically grounded source of external-state and cue mechanisms, formalized those mechanisms, and generated frozen target-blind portfolios. It also tested natural text, simple nulls, paired historical BDD abbreviation, and 20 selective-extraction operators. The final comparison, Task83r, was preregistered and used the deterministic Fingerprint V2.1 refreeze and two independent VM transcriptions.

The central result is:

> `BEST_SUPPORTED_CLASS = INCONCLUSIVE`
>
> `MECHANISM_IDENTIFICATION_FROM_F2 = NOT_IDENTIFIABLE`

Natural text was descriptively closest in the available direct space (distance about `0.675 / 0.684` for ZL3b/IT2a), but `closest` did not mean `supported`: it failed the frozen multi-family gate. Fontana/external-memory outputs retained partial endpoint compatibility (about `1.247 / 1.239`) but only `LEVEL_1` evidence. Phase II therefore did not confirm external memory and did not exclude it globally.

The tested BDD abbreviation tradition was `DISFAVORED`, evidence level `S0`. Its combined real trajectory cosine was only `0.279 / 0.262`, whereas matched deletions were about `0.99`; the minimum shorthand-null p-value was `0.25`. This applies to the tested BDD tradition, not to all historical shorthand.

Selective extraction remained `PARTIAL`, evidence level `A1`. Some operators had directional cosine near `0.80`, but none separated from matched nulls (minimum p-value `0.142857`). FIRST/LAST operators mechanically collapse line structure, and AX diagnostics were not validated. No acrostic or hidden channel was detected.

`NOT_IDENTIFIABLE` does not mean that the hypotheses are equivalent. It means the primary direct model/target intersection contained only **3 of 13 CORE metrics**, all in one edit family. The synthetic token streams did not generate the VM’s hierarchy, locus, folio, page, recto/verso, physical-line, boundary, positional, local-regime, or 2D/manuscript organization. With no class passing its own support gate, pairwise scientific equivalence could not even be tested.

Phase II remains informative because it:

- produced a substantially richer fingerprint whose 13 CORE conclusions are stable across ZL3b and IT2a;
- operationalized external memory as measurable state, knowledge, recovery, ambiguity, and information behavior rather than metaphor;
- demonstrated measurable signatures for historical abbreviation and selective extraction;
- disfavored the tested BDD transformation against matched nulls;
- exposed and corrected a provenance/nondeterminism defect through a byte-identical three-run scientific refreeze;
- located the decisive bottleneck: models currently reproduce a token-stream projection, not a manuscript.

Phase II did not establish that the VM is Fontana’s work, external memory, natural language, cipher, shorthand, acrostic, formal language, or meaningless generation. The full evidence and limitations are in [PHASE2_REPORT.md](PHASE2_REPORT.md).
