# Section-conditioned role protocol

Primary lexical units are exact frozen tokens. Eligible support tiers are frozen as CORE_20 (count≥20, ≥3 sections, ≥5 leaves), CORE_50 (≥50, ≥3 sections, ≥8 leaves), and CORE_100 (≥100, ≥4 sections, ≥10 leaves). Role profiles are fixed before section comparison and separate frequency dimensions from positional, repetition, and predecessor/successor-diversity dimensions.

Section effects are evaluated with physical-leaf-aware aggregation. The primary diagnostic is within-token between-section dispersion of the 16-dimensional role-only profile; frequency-only and combined dimensions are secondary. The structural-class layer is `NOT_APPLICABLE` because no unambiguous frozen page-level class inventory is available. No semantic normalization, token merging, or post-hoc metric selection is performed.
