# Astronomical matching structure audit

## Finding

The frozen split preserves `panel`, Stolfi `group/number`, ZL3b locus, and
absolute transcription position. These are adequate provenance identifiers,
but they are not a geometric annotation: no selected STAR row records angle,
clockwise orientation, ring, adjacency, or independently assigned historical
cluster. Stolfi's comments state only counts such as “29 stars” and “24
stars”; f68r3 contains descriptive Pleiades proximity, but just two of its
selected labels survive the frozen sample and no D1 concept grouping provides
a commensurable partition.

The f67r2 PLANET_MOON comments number pictured moons and attach question-marked
planet guesses. The M1 methodological audit established that these guesses
are not object identifications. Using them to impose the planetary-sphere
order would therefore leak the semantic hypothesis being tested.

Historical order is independently available for the ca. 1400 Oxford rete
(inventory 41468), whose catalogue lists pointers in increasing zodiacal
mediation. What is absent is an independent bridge from that order to any
documented traversal of the selected Voynich STAR labels. Consequently it
cannot license ORDER/CYCLIC/SEQUENCE matching.

## Decision

`ANONYMOUS_SET` and `REUSE_ALLOWED` are applicable. All four models requiring
structural relations are `NOT_APPLICABLE`. This is a data-availability result,
not evidence that the diagrams lack structure.

No geometry was inferred from images, transcription order, token spelling, or
the D1 assignments.
