# Alternative astronomical matching models

The structural audit found no independently documented, commensurable order or grouping for the frozen STAR sample. Stolfi coordinates and transcription positions identify labels but do not establish clockwise/radial traversal. Historical rete order therefore cannot be aligned to them without an arbitrary bridge. ORDER_PRESERVING, CYCLIC_ORDER, GROUP_CONSTRAINED, and SEQUENCE_ALIGNMENT are `NOT_APPLICABLE`.

REUSE_ALLOWED was tested with the complete frozen M1 optimiser and 100 replicates of each search-level null. Its reuse penalty was frozen at 0.05 per assignment beyond the first use of a concept. It achieved 0.600000 TRAIN, 0.000000 HELD_OUT, advantage 0.042000, and p=0.366337; interpretation `STRUCTURE_HURTS`. PLANET_MOON remains exploratory because the diagram positions are not confirmed planet identities.
