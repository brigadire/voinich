# Comparative notation study: goals and guardrails

Status: `EXPERIMENT_PREPARATION_ONLY`  
Authorization: `NO_COMPARATIVE_RESULTS_AUTHORIZED`

The future study asks how closely the structural relations of known notation
systems resemble the frozen structural grammar of the Voynich Manuscript
(VM). It does **not** test whether VM “is” Latin, shorthand, music, a cipher,
or another mechanism. Visual resemblance and symbol names are out of scope.

The five independently reported levels are symbol grammar (G), token
formation (T), sequence grammar (S), observed physical-line grammar (L), and
observed document hierarchy (D). No primary total distance, winner, global
ranking, or post-hoc representation selection is allowed.

The immutable VM reference is `../structure_catalog/`, based on
`../../data_work/ZL3b-x7.canonical.txt` (SHA-256
`f46f4190af65b85d145ec5bb957c1f56029b567e4bef12ac7baa1797f358d692`).
Candidate-specific VM recomputation is forbidden.

This directory contains experiment contracts and fixture-only verification.
It contains no production candidate fingerprint and no VM distance.
