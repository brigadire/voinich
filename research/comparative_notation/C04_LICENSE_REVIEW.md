# C04 licensing review — DECODE historical ciphers

Review date: 2026-08-30. License decision: `LICENSE_UNRESOLVED`.
First-run decision: `EXCLUDED_LICENSE`; `C04_PRODUCTION_READY=false`.

DECODE exposes historical cipher records under record-level terms. The audit
did not establish one uniform grant covering download, local storage,
normalization, machine analysis, derived USC creation, and derived-corpus
redistribution for a frozen monoalphabetic, homophonic, or nomenclator subset.
Website availability is not treated as a corpus license.

| Use | Decision |
|---|---|
| Browse record metadata | Available. |
| Download a frozen mechanism-specific bulk subset | Not established uniformly. |
| Local production analysis | Not authorized without record-level review. |
| Derived USC creation/redistribution | Not established. |
| Derived aggregate statistics | Not sufficient to cure input-use uncertainty. |

No DECODE material was downloaded into the production bundle. Mechanism and
key strata also remain unfrozen, but the unresolved rights decision alone
requires exclusion from the first run. This decision predates all comparative
metrics and may be revisited only through a new source/license freeze.

Source reviewed: `https://de-crypt.org/decrypt-web/RecordsList` (accessed
2026-08-30).
