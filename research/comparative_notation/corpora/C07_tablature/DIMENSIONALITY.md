# C07 dimensionality and token justification

Course position and fret sign jointly carry meaning. TAB-R1 therefore preserves
a complete simultaneous group as the generic token and serializes its
course–fret components in frozen ascending-course order. TAB-R2 is an explicit
sensitivity representation: it emits separate course events but carries
`simultaneity_group` on every event. Rhythmic signs are attributes and symbols
only when visibly repeated in the selected source convention. Traditions are
never merged before analysis.
