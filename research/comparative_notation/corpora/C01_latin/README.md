# C01 — Ordinary historical Latin

Require a scholarly diplomatic or expanded Latin manuscript transcription,
preferably near the fifteenth century. Preserve source word, physical-line,
folio, and section boundaries; absent layout is NULL. Primary candidates are
BDD expanded TEI and stratified CATMuS. Representations: LATIN-DIPLOMATIC and
LATIN-EXPANDED when aligned. Linear, line-structured, and hierarchical only to
the extent encoded by the source.
