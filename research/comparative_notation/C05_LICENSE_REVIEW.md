# C05 licensing review — Fontana, BnF NAL 635

Review date: 2026-08-30. License decision: `LICENSE_UNRESOLVED` for the
required full transcription. First-run decision: `EXCLUDED_LICENSE`;
`C05_PRODUCTION_READY=false`.

The manuscript facsimile (`https://gallica.bnf.fr/ark:/12148/btv1b10023795x`)
is a Gallica reproduction of public-domain manuscript BnF NAL 635. BnF states
that non-commercial reuse and academic/scientific publication are free with
source acknowledgement; commercial reuse generally requires authorization
and a fee.

| Use | Result |
|---|---|
| Download / local research storage | Allowed for non-commercial research with attribution. |
| Normalization and machine analysis | Allowed locally for this academic research purpose. |
| Derived statistics | May be published with attribution. |
| Facsimile redistribution | Not included in this bundle; Gallica conditions continue to apply. |
| Scholarly transcription redistribution | **Not resolved.** Kranz (2016) and Battisti/Saccaro (1984) are separately published editions; no license granting corpus derivation/redistribution was found and no complete licensed transcription is locally acquired. |
| Derived USC corpus | Blocked because it would necessarily encode an unlicensed/unacquired transcription. |

Therefore licensing of the primary images is not the remaining blocker, but
the absence of a rights-cleared complete diplomatic transcription is.

The image permission does not extend the separately published scholarly
transcriptions. The first production run therefore excludes C05 rather than
performing local-only analysis on an unacquired or incompletely licensed text.
