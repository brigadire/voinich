# M1 comparison with M0

M0's frozen maximum was 0/20 TRAIN and 0/6 HELD_OUT. M1 adds only a global
one-or-two-EVA-unit substitution table and obtains 10/20
TRAIN (0.500000) and 0/6 HELD_OUT
(0.000000) for its top observed model. The raw TRAIN gain
over M0 is 0.500000.

This gain cannot be interpreted without the search-level null. The most
conservative mean of the three null maximum-coverages is
0.461500; the best model's advantage is
0.038500, and its conservative empirical p is
0.821782. Each null replicate reran the same bounded M1 search.
M0 artifacts were neither rewritten nor replaced.
