# Results report

The compact path-selection model is implemented with required mappings, forbidden source units, global injective mapping, selected-token support, and independent full scorer replay. Small exhaustive validation and the non-hereditary quadruple regression pass.

The real 25,517-path model did not complete construction/solve within the available resource budget. No incumbent or certified upper bound was produced. Therefore the corrected maximum remains unknown and no threshold is interpreted as infeasible. E3 and null remain disabled.
