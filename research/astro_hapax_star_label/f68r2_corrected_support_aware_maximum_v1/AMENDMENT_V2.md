# Amendment V2: support-aware maximum

This package leaves the prior amendment unchanged. It records the corrected provenance and removes every inference from a lower-coverage subset bound.

Coverage 1, 2, and 3 are independently certified infeasible for the frozen corrected graph. The coverage-3 enumeration is complete. This does not constrain coverage 4 or higher: support is a property of the complete selected set and is not hereditary under subsets.

The pairwise graph is used only for mapping, identity, LABEL, token, required/forbidden, and scorer compatibility. Support remains a set-level constraint and is checked by the support-aware search.
