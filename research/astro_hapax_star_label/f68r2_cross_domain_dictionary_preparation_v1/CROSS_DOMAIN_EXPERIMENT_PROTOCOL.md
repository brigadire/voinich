# Cross-domain experiment protocol

This package defines preparation only; no real cross-domain search is executed.

The future comparison will use the already completed astronomical baseline (maximum 3/27), a botanical corpus frozen only after line-level provenance review, historical non-domain controls, and deterministic orthographic pseudo-lexicon controls. The same 27 f68r2 occurrences, 64 frozen profiles, global type-based generator, functional/injective mapping, DROP_UNMAPPED semantics, GLOBAL_CAPACITY_1, uncapped global mapping table, and support threshold of two distinct EVA token types must be used in every domain.

Before search, freeze:

- full corpus sizes and panel IDs;
- canonical identity and historical-form multiplicity;
- length, character, repetition, language, and chronology matching rules;
- profile order and solver budget;
- the primary metric: certified maximum support-valid coverage across all 64 profiles;
- the number of botanical and control panels;
- the empirical null/randomization procedure;
- multiplicity correction across all domains, panels, and model-selection outcomes.

A botanical result is eligible for further comparison only if it reaches at least 4/27, exceeds the astronomical 3/27 baseline, and passes model–scorer parity. This is a screening criterion, not a significance claim. Any final claim must compare the full model-selection maxima for astronomy, botany, historical controls, and pseudo-controls. Choosing the best botanical panel after seeing results is prohibited.
