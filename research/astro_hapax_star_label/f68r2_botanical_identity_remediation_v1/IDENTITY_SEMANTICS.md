# Identity semantics

`SOURCE_CONCEPT_ID` denotes one independently distinguishable botanical concept as named and described by Isidore XVII. It is not a modern biological species and no binomial attribution is made.

Forms are merged only by explicit source equivalence, explicit alternative naming, inflectional identity, or a separately frozen line-level authority. Similarity, subtype language, shared genus wording, and modern intuition do not merge concepts. The 89 admitted forms here have one source-entry concept each; no unproved synonym collapse is performed. The 45 rejected rows are varieties/epithets, fruits, or products/materials.

Controls use the identical rule: one independently locatable technical source entry per concept, with no EVA or coverage-dependent identity decisions.
