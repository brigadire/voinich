# Support-definition erratum

The original E1 support audit counted two occurrences of the EVA token `hy` as two independent pairs. The corrected rule is `INDEPENDENT_SUPPORT_REQUIRES_DISTINCT_EVA_TYPES=YES`; repeated occurrences of one EVA type do not count independently. Therefore the raw 2/57 E1 incumbent remains recorded, while its support-valid coverage under the corrected gate is 0/57. The E1 solver and raw results are unchanged.
