# E2 exact expansion

E2 expanded the exact global injective `DROP_UNMAPPED` search to table sizes 5 and 6. Reachability increased from 15/57 in E1 to 35/57 at k=5 and 47/57 at k=6. This is a structural reachability result only.

The four solver configurations (two table sizes × two capacity modes) were run with 60-second budgets and complete E1 warm-start tables. All returned `UNKNOWN` without an incumbent under the selected budget. Consequently E2 produced no support-valid candidate coverage and no optimum certificate. Top-K and co-optimal enumeration were not performed.

No local errors, digraphs, abbreviations, compositions, E3, or E4 modes were executed. The result is exploratory and makes no astronomical identification or decipherment claim.
