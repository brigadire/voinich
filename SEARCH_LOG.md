This audit treats a source as evidence only where its original publication or official author repository was located. Abstract-only records are catalogued but excluded from detailed control claims.

## Inclusion and exclusion

Included: peer-reviewed journal/conference papers, preprints where a reviewed version was not found, historical primary material, and official reproducibility repositories. Excluded as evidence: forums, blogs, Wikipedia, press coverage, unvalidated claimed decipherments, and papers for which a method could not be checked beyond an abstract. Claim-level entries identify `FULL_TEXT_UNAVAILABLE` rather than reconstructing methods from summaries.

The starting names Currier, Friedman, Landini, Stolfi, Schinner, Timm, Montemurro–Zanette, Reddy–Knight, Bowern/Lindemann, Greshko/Naibbe, Rozanova–Temerev, Averyanov, and Hermes/Polygraphia were searched. No eligible primary computational Voynich publication by Averyanov, nor a directly relevant primary “Hermes” item, was located in the searched records; neither absence is used as evidence.
